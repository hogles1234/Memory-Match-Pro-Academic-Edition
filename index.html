📖 How to Play

🎯 Goal: Flip cards two at a time to find matching pairs. The faster you match all pairs, the better your score!
🚀 Before the Game Starts
  1. Enter your name and the Session ID your instructor gave you, then tap Join.
  2. Wait for your instructor to launch the session from their dashboard.
  3. A Pre-Game Exposure phase will begin — all cards flip face-up for a few seconds so you can study them. Pay close attention!
  4. When the timer runs out the cards flip back over and the game begins.
🃏 Playing the Game
  1. Click any face-down card to flip it over.
  2. Click a second card to see if it matches the first.
  3. If they match ✔ Match! — both cards stay face-up and your score goes up.
  4. If they don't match ✘ Try again — both cards flip back over after a short pause. Remember where they were!
  5. Keep going until all pairs are matched to complete the level.
📊 Your Stats
⏱ TimeHow many seconds you've spent on this level.
🔀 MovesTotal number of card pairs you've flipped.
🎯 AccuracyCorrect matches ÷ total moves × 100. Aim for 100%!
🏆 LevelYour current level out of the total set by your instructor.
💡 Tips for Students
Study during Exposure! Cards are shown for a short time before the game — look at them carefully. Try grouping them in your mind (e.g. "the animal was in the top-left corner").
Don't rush. Fewer, better moves = higher accuracy. It's better to think before clicking than to guess randomly.
Difficulty changes the board. Easy uses 4 pairs (8 cards), Medium uses 6 pairs, Hard uses 8 pairs — and the exposure time gets shorter!
🏅 Difficulty Summary
🟢 Easy4 pairs · Long exposure · Great for beginners
🟡 Medium6 pairs · Medium exposure · A real challenge
🔴 Hard8 pairs · Short exposure · Test your memory!
🔄 RestartYou can restart a level anytime — but it adds to your restart count!
🎯 Goal: Cards are shuffled on screen. Tap them in the correct order (1st → 2nd → 3rd …). One wrong tap and that card shakes — keep trying until you get the sequence right!
🚀 Before the Game Starts
  1. Enter your name and the Session ID, then tap Join.
  2. Your instructor will choose Sequential Mode when setting up the session.
  3. A Pre-Game Exposure phase shows the cards in the correct order for a few seconds — memorise the sequence!
  4. When the timer ends the cards are shuffled and placed face-up in random positions.
🔢 Playing Sequential Mode
  1. All cards are visible but in a random order on the board.
  2. Tap the card that belongs in position 1 first.
  3. If correct ✔ — a numbered badge appears on the card and it locks in place.
  4. If wrong ✘ Shake! — the card shakes. No penalty is added immediately; just try again.
  5. Continue tapping cards in order (2nd, 3rd, 4th …) until all are placed.
  6. Completing the full sequence finishes the level and you advance!
🗂️ What Kind of Sequences Are There?
🔢 Number PatternsPut numbers in ascending or pattern order (e.g. 2, 4, 6, 8…)
🌈 Rainbow / Color OrderArrange colors from light → dark or ROYGBIV order.
🐾 Smallest → BiggestOrder animals or shapes by size.
🔤 A → Z OrderingAlphabetical sequences of animal names, color names, etc.
📐 Shape ComplexitySimple shapes → more complex shapes.
🌡️ Warm → Cool ColorsArrange colors by temperature: red → orange → yellow → blue.
📊 Your Stats in Sequential Mode
⏱ TimeTotal seconds used for this level.
🔀 MovesEvery tap counts — correct and incorrect.
🎯 AccuracyCorrect taps ÷ total taps × 100. Try not to guess!
🏆 LevelCurrent level out of your session total.
💡 Tips for Sequential Mode
Use the Exposure phase! The sequence is shown in order before cards are shuffled. Say it silently to yourself: "Chicken → Sheep → Pig → Cow."
Look for clues on the card. Each card shows a word, emoji, or both. If you remember the category (e.g. "smallest animal first") that narrows it down fast.
Wrong taps shake, not fail. A wrong tap makes the card shake but you can keep tapping. You won't lose the level — but each wrong tap lowers your accuracy!
Difficulty changes sequence length. Easy = 4 steps  |  Medium = 6 steps  |  Hard = 8 steps
👩‍🏫 You control the session. Set the category, difficulty, game mode, and number of levels — then share the Session ID with your students and launch when ready.
⚙️ Setting Up a Session
  1. From the main menu tap ▶ Start, then choose Instructor.
  2. Pick a Game Mode: 🧩 Memory Match or 🔢 Sequential.
  3. Select your Category (Math, Shapes, Colors, Animals) and Difficulty (Easy / Medium / Hard).
  4. Set the number of levels (1–10) and an optional accuracy baseline (alert threshold).
  5. A unique Session ID is generated — share it with your students verbally or on the board.
  6. Wait on the Live Dashboard until all students have joined, then tap 🚀 Launch.
📊 Live Dashboard
👤 NameStudent's entered name
🏆 LevelCurrent level the student is on
🔀 MovesTotal card taps / pair flips made
⏱ TimeElapsed time for that student
🎯 AccuracyLive accuracy %. Turns red if below baseline
🚩 FlagAuto-alert when accuracy drops below your set threshold
📥 Exporting Results
When the session ends click 📥 Export CSV on the dashboard. The file includes each student's name, level reached, total moves, time, accuracy, restart count, and flag status — ready for your records.
💡 Instructor Tips
Choosing the right mode: Use Memory Match to reinforce concept pairs (e.g. "3 + 1 = 4"). Use Sequential Mode to practice ordering skills (alphabetical order, number patterns, size ordering).
Set a realistic baseline. For first attempts try 60–70%. Raise it as students improve. The flag will alert you to students who need extra support.
Exposure time is automatic: Easy = 4 s · Medium = 2.5 s · Hard = 1.5 s. Choose Hard to push advanced learners.

🌌 About

Memory Match Pro – Academic Edition is a web-based educational game designed for elementary students to reinforce academic concepts through structured card-matching mechanics.

The platform features adaptive difficulty, real-time instructor dashboards, automated intervention alerts, and data export for record-keeping.

Supported Categories: Animals, Shapes, Colors, Mathematics
Version: 1.0 – Academic Prototype
