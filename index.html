📖 How to Play

For Students

  • Enter your name and the Session ID given by your instructor
  • Cards will briefly show during the Pre-Game Exposure phase
  • Click two cards to flip them. If they match, they stay open!
  • Complete all pairs to advance to the next level
  • Match as many pairs as possible and have fun!

For Instructors

  • Choose your academic category, difficulty, and level count
  • Share the Session ID with your students
  • View the Live Dashboard to monitor accuracy in real-time
  • Export results as CSV when the session ends

🌌 About

Memory Match Pro – Academic Edition is a web-based educational game designed for elementary students to reinforce academic concepts through structured card-matching mechanics.

The platform features adaptive difficulty, real-time instructor dashboards, automated intervention alerts, and data export for record-keeping.

Supported Categories: Animals, Shapes, Colors, Mathematics
Version: 1.0 – Academic Prototype
